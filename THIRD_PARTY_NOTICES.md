# Third-party notices

Smallpowers is inspired by and draws heavily from the two upstream projects named below. Many of its workflow and code-quality practices were learned from and adapted from their work.

## Superpowers

Smallpowers adapts software-development workflow concepts from [obra/superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers), version 6.3.0, source revision `b36e0829c6d0140e93cfef2ca599b1b07d4a7797`.

The reviewed source set includes Superpowers' brainstorming, plan-writing, plan execution, parallel-agent, test-driven-development, code-review, completion-verification, Git-worktree isolation, and development-branch completion guidance. Forge-selection behavior was also reviewed from Superpowers history at revision `24ae4c800109494cb2fe06c110dd9f0da023de89`. Smallpowers adapts the decision-changing procedures while retaining its own explicit-only entrypoints, dependency-graph scheduler, approval boundary, transactional workspace model, and bounded-review model.

## Ponytail

Smallpowers adapts minimalism and over-engineering review concepts from [DietrichGebert/ponytail](https://github.com/DietrichGebert/ponytail), version 4.9.0-era source revision `2ed6c52c9d7e5e56942508591085fd45dea277d3`.

The reviewed source set includes Ponytail's implementation, review, audit, deliberate-debt, gain-reporting, and help material. Smallpowers imports the relevant technical discipline—flow comprehension, reuse and native-capability preference, over-engineering detection, semantic-equivalence checks, safety boundaries, measurable shortcut ceilings, and evidence honesty—but not Ponytail's persona, modes, hooks, command surface, marketing language, or branding.
